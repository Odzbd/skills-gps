      How this is measured

      Every week, postings are read from the public job boards of the employers in the project's list. Company boilerplate, duplicates and self-mentions are removed, then skills are matched against a fixed dictionary.

      Demand is the share of postings that ask for a skill. Large employers are damped so a few of them cannot decide the ranking.

      Priority is the average of two numbers: demand in the role, and demand in jobs that already use your skills. With nothing ticked, it equals demand.

      Limits. The employers are tech-forward and mostly in the US and Europe, and only English postings are read. This is not the whole labour market.

      Privacy. Your choices stay in this browser and in the page link. Nothing is sent anywhere.
